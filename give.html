At Hillside, we aim to worship God in our giving by coming together to contribute cheerfully and regularly to the support of the ministry, the expenses of the church, the relief of the poor, and the spread of the gospel through all nations.

2 Corinthians 9:6–7

We strive to honor the Lord with the first-fruits of all our labor.

Proverbs 3:9

May our giving not end with our physical resources, but may we also serve with our talents and time, as we use the gifts God has given each of us for the common good.

Designated funds.

Contributions to existing designated funds are welcome. If the need for the fund has been fully met, or it is determined that it has become impracticable, or a decision has been made to close the fund for any other reason, the church will vote to close the fund. Any remaining balance will be under the discretion of the church.
