The Romans Road

Good news.
For you.

God knows you. God loves you. Discover what the Bible says about a life with Jesus.

A journey through Scripture

Grace changes
everything.

Because Jesus took our place and the punishment for our sin, we can be close to God and have the joy, peace, and goodness that life with Jesus brings.

01

Romans 5:8

God loves you.

Here is how God showed His love for us: While we were still sinners, Jesus Christ died for us.

02

Romans 3:23

We all need grace.

God knows you aren’t perfect. God knows we have all sinned.

03

Romans 6:23a

Sin separates us from God.

Our sin earns death.

04

John 3:16 · Romans 5:8

Jesus took our place.

God loves you so much that He sent Jesus, His only Son, to die in your place.

05

Romans 6:23b

Eternal life is God’s gift.

God’s gift through the perfect sacrifice of Jesus is eternal life.

06

Romans 10:9–10

Trust Jesus as Lord.

God tells us in the Bible that we can turn to Jesus as Lord of our life, believing that God raised Jesus from the dead, and we can trust Jesus with everything in our life.
