Growing faith. Little by little.

Children

Every child is a gift from the Lord, and we want to help families with discipleship and to equip kids for life. We provide opportunities for children to learn about God and the truth of His Word. Children are taught at age appropriate levels in a safe and engaging environment. Children are encouraged to grow through Bible study and activities, to serve others, and to develop a relationship with Jesus Christ.

02

Faith for the everyday.

Youth

The focus of the Youth Ministry is to establish a foundation for building Christian character, morals, values and behavior in the young people of our community. Our vision is to respond to the social and spiritual needs of the youth within our church and community. Youth are encouraged to get involved in the community of the church and to grow in their understanding and application of Scripture. There are plenty of ways to learn, serve, and have fun at Hillside.

Young adults (YAM)

As college students and young adults, we are seeking to serve the Lord and connect with one another. We meet regularly to fellowship, play games, and encourage one another.

The Young Adult Ministry (YAM) builds relationships, encourages one another in Christ, and serves together. Join us for games, coffee, and fellowship. Whether you are single, dating, or married, come hang out with us.

Adults
