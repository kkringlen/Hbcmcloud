This is Hillside

Rooted in faith.
Growing in community.

Our story began in 1961, when a group of Christ followers planted a church in the Hillside community north of McLoud, Oklahoma.

Who are we?

Following Jesus.
Living life together.

Our story as a body of believers begins as each person trusts in Jesus Christ as Savior and Lord above all. We are called to live our lives in community, to learn and grow together, to build one another up, and to become more and more devoted to following Christ.

We are not perfect, and all of us have, at one time or another, been strangers, new to the community of faith. Because of Christ, we are family, we are community, and we are living out life together.

Our mission

Devoted Christ followers.
Good news for the world.

Our mission is to build a community of devoted Christ Followers. We seek to glorify God by making disciples through the Gospel of Jesus Christ. As those who have been reconciled to God through Jesus Christ, we are now ambassadors of reconciliation to a lost and broken world. We gather, teach, disciple, and send to see God glorified through reconciliation.

What we believe

Our foundation is Jesus Christ.

Though times have changed from the beginnings of this country church, the message of Jesus Christ will always stay the same.

At Hillside, we adhere to the inerrant Word of God which is preserved by God for all people today, and the foundational statements of faith found in the Baptist Faith & Message.

Read the Baptist Faith & Message ↗

“For there is one God and one mediator between God and humanity, the man Christ Jesus, who gave himself as a ransom for all, a testimony at the proper time.”

I Timothy 2:5–6

We believe that God is one God who knows us each completely and showed His love in this way: While we were still sinners, Christ died for our sins. Jesus Christ was buried and He was raised to life on the third day. You can trust Jesus as your living Savior and Lord because He lives forever.

Romans 5:8 · I Corinthians 15:3–4 · Hebrews 7:24

We believe in the importance of the local body of believers. As fellow Christ Followers, we are called to worship God together, build up one another in Christ, and take seriously our mission to share the message of Christ in the broken world, baptize and disciple those who repent from sin and turn to Christ as Lord, and meet people in whatever circumstance with the truth and love of the Gospel, to the glory of God.

Mission minded

A heart for our neighbors.
And the world.

At Hillside, we believe in giving back. Thanks to your generous giving, we give directly to the Cooperative Program supporting local, national and international missions. There are also opportunities throughout the year to participate in missions, allowing us to obey God’s call to pray for missionaries, to give toward mission work, and to go on mission trips.
